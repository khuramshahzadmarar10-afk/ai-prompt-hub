About AI Prompt Hub

AI Prompt Hub is a free AI creator toolkit built for the worldwide creator community — YouTubers, Reels and TikTok creators, AI filmmakers, designers and marketers.

It packs five focused tools into one fast page:

  • AI Video Prompt Generator — turn plain ideas into professional video-generation prompts.
  • AI Image Prompt Generator — craft detailed image prompts in any art style.
  • Viral Titles + Hashtags — click-worthy titles and optimized hashtag sets.
  • Shorts Script Outline — hook → beats → CTA structures for short-form video.
  • Thumbnail Text Ideas — short high-CTR text lines for thumbnails.

Everything runs 100% in your browser using built-in templates — no signup, no API keys, no usage limits, and nothing you type is uploaded anywhere.
